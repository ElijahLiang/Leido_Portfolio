DESIGN MANIFESTO · TORCH OF LIGHT

I have always believed—
The meaning of life lies in how people illuminate one another.
Design, technology, and art are the torches that carry that light forward.

Since childhood, I have been deeply moved by the inequalities of the world—by how many gentle souls endure pain, and how fragility and resilience coexist within life itself. I began to wonder: how might design and technology help the unseen be equally seen, and the unheard be gently understood?

Five years ago, I shifted from business to design. That year, under the lanterns of Nanjing's Confucius Temple, I made a wish:
"To become a designer who can bring happiness to others."

After five years of practice, I've realized that happiness cannot be given—it can only be empowered. True design is about creating spaces, tools, and systems through which individuals can rediscover their innate strength.
I hope my design allows every form of life—
human or non-human—
to be gently seen and kindly treated in this world.
